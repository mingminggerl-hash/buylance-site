This Privacy Policy explains what information Buylance.gg ("Buylance," "we," "us") collects when you browse the Site, place an order, or contact support, and how we use it.

1. What We Collect

  • Contact details — name, email, and any info you give us to complete an order
  • Order info — items purchased, order history, and any notes you send with an order
  • Delivery info — your Roblox username and anything else needed to hand off your order
  • Payment confirmation — we don't store your card or payment details ourselves; payments are handled by our payment providers (like PayPal), not us directly
  • Support messages — anything you send us through the feedback forms or support channels
  • Basic browsing data — things like your browser type and pages viewed, which most websites collect automatically

2. How We Use It

  • To process and deliver your order
  • To respond to support requests and resolve issues
  • To prevent fraud and keep the Site secure
  • To improve the Site and understand what's working

We don't sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Who We Share It With

We only share what's needed to actually run the store — our payment provider (to process your payment), and any tool we use to host the Site or manage support forms. We don't share your info for advertising purposes.

4. Cookies

The Site may use basic cookies to remember preferences and understand traffic. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

5. Data Retention

We keep order and support records for as long as reasonably needed to resolve disputes, maintain records, and comply with any legal requirements.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of storing or sending data online is 100% secure — please avoid sending sensitive info (like passwords) through unofficial channels. We will never ask for your Roblox password.

7. Children's Privacy

If you're under the age of majority where you live, please only use the Site with a parent or guardian's supervision.

8. Your Choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting support.

Contact for privacy requests: buylancegg@gmail.com

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally — the "Last Updated" date reflects the current version.
