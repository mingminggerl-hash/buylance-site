Rules & Regulations

These are the ground rules for using Buylance.gg — for both buyers and support conversations. Breaking them can lead to account restrictions, as described below.

1. Be Respectful

  • No harassment, threats, or abusive language toward support or anyone helping run the store
  • No spamming support with repeated messages about the same issue — one clear message gets you a reply faster than five angry ones
  • Frustration happens, and that's okay — just keep it civil. We're a small team doing our best to help.

2. Be Honest

  • No fraud, fake payment claims, or chargeback abuse
  • No impersonating another buyer or misrepresenting an order
  • Give us accurate info (especially your Roblox username) so we can actually deliver your order

3. No Exploiting the System

  • No abusing the free spin draw or cashback system (e.g. fake accounts to enter multiple times)
  • No attempting to manipulate or exploit checkout, pricing, or delivery in bad faith

4. What Happens If You Break the Rules

We use a 3-strike system. Warnings are issued at our discretion after reviewing a report, and expire automatically after 90 days of clean behavior — they're not permanent marks.

1st Warning: A reminder. Your account and balance are unaffected — this is just a heads-up that something needs to change.
2nd Warning: You lose access to the $50 spin feature until this strike expires or is cleared.
3rd Warning: Your account is frozen for 1 month — no withdrawals, deposits, or new orders during that time. This is not permanent; full access is automatically restored once the month is up.

If you think an action was taken against you wrongfully, email support and explain your side — your balance stays in place while we review it. This is the same policy laid out in our Terms of Service.
