Refund Policy

This Refund Policy explains when Buylance.gg may issue a refund. It should be read together with our Terms of Service. Since we sell digital items and currency, the rules below reflect the nature of that kind of product.

1. Final After Delivery

Once an order is delivered to your account, it's final and can't be refunded — digital items can't be "returned" the way a physical product can.

2. When a Refund May Be Given

  • Non-delivery — you never received your item and we can't complete delivery within a reasonable time after you contact us
  • Wrong item delivered — we'll correct it first; if that's not possible, we'll refund it
  • Duplicate charge — a verified double-charge on the same order
  • Order cancelled before delivery — if we can't fulfill it, we'll refund you

3. Unclaimed Orders

If you don't respond or complete the steps needed for us to deliver your order (like confirming your Roblox username), we'll treat it as unclaimed after 14 days from purchase. At that point, it converts automatically to store credit for the same amount, usable on a future Buylance order. If you'd rather have it refunded to your original payment method instead, email support with your order details and we'll sort it out.

4. When Refunds Are Not Given

  • The order was already delivered
  • You gave us the wrong Roblox username
  • You didn't respond to our delivery instructions in a reasonable time
  • Roblox-side restrictions (bans, trade cooldowns, account limits) blocked delivery — this is outside our control
  • Simple change of mind after delivery
  • We reasonably believe the request involves fraud or refund abuse

5. How to Request Help

Contact support with your order details, your Roblox username, and a clear description of the issue. Please reach out within 48 hours of the delivery time or purchase (whichever is later) — requests after that are harder for us to verify.

Support contact: buylancegg@gmail.com

6. Chargebacks

If something's wrong, please contact us before filing a chargeback with your bank. Chargebacks on delivered orders may result in account restriction, and we may share order records with the payment provider to contest it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally — the "Last Updated" date reflects the current version.
